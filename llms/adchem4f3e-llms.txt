## What the company sells

- High-purity silicon-based precursors: BDEAS, BTBAS, DIPAS, DIS
- High-K precursors: TDMAHf, TEMAHf, CpHf, HfCl4, TEMAZ, CpZr, ZrCl4, Star-Ti
- Metal and conductive-film precursors: TMA, La(FAMD)3, TBTDEN, CpNb, WCl5, MoCl5, MoO2Cl2,
  CpCo(CO)2, CCTBA, Ru(EtCp)2
- Perovskite photovoltaic precursors
- Custom source canisters (源瓶定制), liquid delivery systems, thin-film process solutions
